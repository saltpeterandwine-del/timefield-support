Timefield Support · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026. This policy applies to the Timefield iOS app, operated by its developer, Zhang Xinyue (张馨悦). Contact: 940644554@qq.com.

Time records stay on your device

Your time entries, activities, mixed activities, notes, tags, and optional Meaning and Happiness ratings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Timefield does not send this life-record content to its developer or RevenueCat. There is no Timefield account or app cloud-sync service in this version.

Optional purchases

Apple processes payments. Timefield uses RevenueCat to validate purchase status, manage Pro access, and support purchase restoration. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase and transaction information needed for these services and purchase analytics. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Your timeline, activities, notes, tags, and ratings are not sent to RevenueCat.

We do not use purchase information for cross-app advertising tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy for their practices.

Permissions and advertising

This version does not request microphone, camera, notification, or advertising-tracking permission. It does not include advertising or social sharing of your records.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to answer and resolve your request. Avoid sending private records unless you choose to do so.

Control of your information

You can edit or delete records within the app. Removing the app may remove local records. Restoring a purchase restores Pro access, not local records. Contact support for requests about information supplied in support correspondence. Apple controls payment records and refund requests.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages contain no added analytics scripts, advertising, or account forms. The hosting provider may process normal website request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Policy updates

We may update this policy if app functionality or service practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
