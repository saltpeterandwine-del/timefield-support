Timefield Support

Timefield is a visual time tracker. Record activities, keep overlapping activities together, and look back at a colored daily timeline.

Contact

For help, email 940644554@qq.com. Include your app version, iOS version, and a brief description. Do not send passwords, payment details, or private timeline data.

Start and stop a record

On Today, use the record control or pull down within the timeline, choose a category and activity, then Start. Stop recording saves your record before the optional memory sheet. You may keep or discard a very short record.

Correct or add past time

Tap a saved record to inspect and edit it. Tap a blank gap to fill in past time, or use Options > Add past time. Mixed records have one Main activity and up to four secondary activities; the time is counted once.

Timefield Pro

Options > Settings > Upgrade to Pro opens the lifetime purchase. Pro unlocks Weekly Insights, main-category breakdown, and Forest, Dusk, and Morning Mist themes. Recording and daily history remain free. Apple displays the price for your region.

Restore a lifetime purchase

Use Options > Settings > Restore Purchases, or Restore Purchases on the Timefield Pro screen. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Restoration restores Pro access; it does not restore local time records.

If a purchase is unavailable or pending, check your internet connection and App Store account, then try again later or contact support. A cancelled or failed purchase does not remove your records. For a refund, use Apple's purchase support.

Your local records

No Timefield account or cloud sync is required. Records are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove its local records; purchase restoration is separate from record storage.
